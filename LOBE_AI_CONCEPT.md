## Executive Summary

Lobe is an AI-powered, screenless consumer device focused on **dream capture and completion**. The device passively listens during sleep, integrates with biometric trackers, and helps users recall and understand their dreams.

---

## Product Vision

### The Problem
- Dreams are fragmented and difficult to explain after waking
- "Dream residue" fades rapidly - within minutes of waking, recall deteriorates significantly
- Users want to share meaningful dream experiences with partners/family but struggle to articulate them
- The market for daytime listening/transcription is saturated (Granola, Notion AI notes, etc.)

### The Solution
Lobe is a passive listening device that:
1. Monitors when you enter REM sleep (via biometric integration)
2. Records audio during REM sleep when dreams occur
3. Captures your morning stream-of-consciousness recall
4. Combines all data to "complete" your dream narrative

## Technical Architecture

### Input Sources
1. **Passive Audio Recording**
   - Records during REM sleep periods
   - Captures sleep talking, ambient sounds
   - No wake word required (truly passive)

2. **Biometric Integration**
   - Fitbit
   - Oura Ring
   - Apple Watch
   - Garmin
   - Other fitness/sleep trackers

3. **Active Audio Input**
   - Morning dream recall from user
   - Stream-of-consciousness speaking upon waking

### Processing
- Detect REM sleep onset via biometric data triggers
- Correlate biometric patterns with audio recordings
- AI processing to synthesize:
  - Passive overnight recordings
  - Biometric sleep data
  - User's morning recall
- Generate coherent dream narrative

### Output
- Audio playback of completed dream narrative
- Shareable with family members (multi-device support)

## Technical Challenges to Solve

1. **REM Detection Accuracy**
   - Reliance on consumer biometric devices
   - Latency in triggering recording

2. **Audio Quality**
   - Nightstand placement vs. pillow proximity
   - Background noise filtering
   - Sleep talking clarity

3. **Dream Completion Algorithm**
   - How to synthesize fragmentary data into coherent narrative
   - Balancing accuracy vs. confabulation
   - Handling users who don't sleep talk

4. **Privacy Concerns**
   - Always-on bedroom recording
   - Data storage and security
   - Multi-person households

5. **Hardware Design**
   - Screenless interaction patterns
   - Indicator lights/sounds
   - Partner-friendly (non-intrusive)
